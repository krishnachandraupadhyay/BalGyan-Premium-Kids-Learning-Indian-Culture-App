# Walkthrough: BalGyan — Premium Kids Learning & Indian Culture Web Application

The **BalGyan** interactive educational web application for children aged **3–8 years** is fully built and running locally. The platform turns learning into a joyful adventure using the **Picture → Interaction → Voice → Learning → Reward** methodology.

---

## 🌟 What Was Built & Features Included

### 1. 🔤 English Adventure (130+ Words with Dual Audio)
- **A–Z Coverage**: All 26 letters from A to Z with **at least 5 distinct words per letter (130+ structured words)** including Apple, Ant, Airplane, Alligator, Arrow, Ball, Cat, Dog, Elephant, etc.
- **Dual Case Support**: Large instant toggle between **🅰️ CAPITAL LETTERS** and **🅰️ small letters**.
- **Voice & Translation**: Tapping any picture pronounces the letter and word in child-friendly voice (e.g., *"A for Apple. Apple means सेब"*) with Hindi translation badges and animated rewards.
- **Randomization Engine**: Non-repetitive random word rotation every time a letter is visited.
- **Game Modes**:
  - **Picture Match**: *"Find the picture for R"* with 3 large interactive choices (🌹 Rose, 🐱 Cat, 🍎 Apple).
  - **Letter Recognition**: *"Which one is A?"* with encouraging feedback.

### 2. 🕉️ Hindi Garden (हिंदी वाटिका)
- **Swar (स्वर)**: All 13 vowels (अ से अः) with interactive fruit/nature pictures and bilingual pronunciation.
- **Vyanjan (व्यंजन)**: All 36 consonants (क से ज्ञ) with words (कमल, खरगोश, गमला, etc.).
- **Interactive Quizzes**: "अक्षर पहचान" quiz with rewards and encouragement.

### 3. 🔢 Number World (संख्या लोक 1-100)
- **Touch & Count Mode**: Clickable/touchable fruit & toy items that pop with real-time counting sounds.
- **"How Many?" Visual Quiz**: Count items on screen and pick the correct number.
- **1 to 100 Chart**: Interactive number grid pronouncing names in both English and Hindi.

### 4. ➕ Math Lab (गणित लैब)
- **Visual Addition**: $🍎 + 🍎🍎 = 3$ (Visual object counters shown before mathematical notation).
- **Visual Subtraction**: $🎈🎈🎈 - 🎈 = 2$
- **Visual Multiplication & Division**: Cookie packs and sharing pizzas among cute animal friends.

### 5. 🧗 Table Mountain (पहाड़ा पर्वत)
- **Mountain Climbing Game**: Tables 2 through 20.
- **Gamified Ascent**: Answering each table multiplication question climbs 1 of 10 mountain checkpoints, with the child's mascot moving up in real time to reach the **Summit Peak 🚩** and unlock trophies.

### 6. 🕉️ Sanskrit Gurukul (संस्कृत गुरुकुल)
- **35+ Vocabulary Words**: Animals (पशवः), Nature (प्रकृतिः), Family (परिवारः), and Sanskrit Numbers (एकम्, द्वे, त्रीणि...).
- **Sacred Shlokas**: Gayatri Mantra, Saraswati Vandana, Guru Vandana, Morning Shloka (*Karaagre Vasate Lakshmi*), and Shanti Mantra with Devanagari lyrics, audio recitation, and child-friendly meanings.

### 7. 🇮🇳 Bharat Explorer & National Anthem
- **10 National Symbols**: Tiranga Flag, Lion Capital Emblem, Royal Bengal Tiger, Indian Peacock, Lotus, Banyan Tree, Mango, Ganga, Rupee, and Elephant.
- **Cultural Heritage**: Famous Monuments (Taj Mahal, Red Fort, Hawa Mahal, Konark), Classical Dances (Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Garba, Bhangra), and Musical Instruments (Sitar, Tabla, Flute, Veena).
- **Anthem & Song Player**: Full respect-first player for **जन गण मन (Jana Gana Mana)** and **वन्दे मातरम् (Vande Mataram)** with bouncing line-by-line highlight and meaning.

### 8. 🪔 Indian Festivals (त्योहार मेला)
- **8 Major Festivals**: Diwali, Holi, Ganesh Chaturthi, Janmashtami, Raksha Bandhan, Makar Sankranti, Eid-ul-Fitr, and National Days.
- **Interactive Mini-Games**: Light 5 Diyas, Pop 5 color balloons, Collect 5 modaks, Fly 5 kites!

### 9. 💚 Good Habits Garden (अच्छी आदतें)
- **10 Core Values**: Respecting elders, cleanliness, saving water, sharing toys, caring for animals, planting trees, polite speech (*Please & Thank You*), healthy eating, sleeping on time, and patriotism.
- **Interactive Moral Choice Dilemmas**: Choices like *Help vs Ignore* with gentle positive reinforcement.

### 10. 📖 Story World (कथा संसार)
- **5 Illustrated Multi-page Stories**:
  1. *आरव और जादुई पेड़* (Aarav & The Magic Tree)
  2. *चतुर खरगोश और शेर* (The Clever Rabbit)
  3. *सच्चे दोस्त: कबूतर और चींटी* (The Dove & The Ant)
  4. *पानी की नन्ही बूँद "टिप-टिप"* (The Little Raindrop)
  5. *मोर और कौवे का सबक* (The Peacock & The Crow)
- Interactive page flip actions + moral lessons + post-story quizzes.

### 11. ✍️ Tracing Canvas (अक्षर आलेखन)
- Dotted guide letter outlines for English Capital (A-Z), Small (a-z), Hindi (अ, आ, क...), and Numbers (1-10).
- Smooth HTML5 canvas drawing, color palettes, eraser, and star completion celebration.

### 12. 🎮 Arcade Hub, 👤 Avatar Shop & 🏆 Badges
- **Bubble Pop**: Pop floating balloons matching the target letter.
- **Memory Card Match**: Flip cards to pair letters with pictures.
- **Avatar Dress-Up Shop**: 6 Cartoon Mascots (Chintu Tiger, Golu Elephant, Meera, Kabir, Pari Peacock, Sheru Lion) + Hats (Crowns, Turbans, Caps) + Glasses + Companion Pets unlockable with earned coins!
- **Badges**: ABC Explorer, Hindi Hero, Math Master, Sanskrit Acharya, Little Patriot, etc.

### 13. 📊 Parent Dashboard & 📝 Admin CMS
- **Parent Gate**: Simple math challenge to ensure children do not alter settings.
- **Analytics**: Total learning time, accuracy rate, subject mastery bars, and adaptive recommendations.
- **Admin Content CMS**: Dynamic form to add/edit new words and questions + Export/Import content as JSON.

## 🚀 How to Run & Access Locally

The dev server is currently live:
- **Local URL**: `http://localhost:3000/`
- Command to run whenever needed: `npm run dev` in the project root.
